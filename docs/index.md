# Introduction

!!! abstract "Serial Communications"

These notes on serial data communications are intended to introduce some practical applications of the technology in the early part of a module in data networking. These notes are an introduction and are intended to let us share a basis of terminology and understanding. 

It may seem anachronistic to be studying a technology that dates back to the earliest days of computing. However, this remains the technology we use every day. In these notes I'm going to focus on old fashioned Serial! In your own time, do a little reading with respect to USB. You will see that many of the principles are exactly the same, with a very different electrical interface.
